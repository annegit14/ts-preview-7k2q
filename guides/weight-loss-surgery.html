Traveling abroad for weight-loss surgery

Gastric sleeve, gastric bypass and other bariatric procedures: what to know before you book your trip.

The care you need and the getaway you want, both in one destination.

Why choose medical travel?

Many people choose weight-loss surgery abroad for the licensed doctors and accredited facilities, shorter wait times, and lower costs than they might pay at home.

A gastric sleeve surgery in Turkey can cost $4,500 or less,* compared with $15,000 or more in the U.S.†

International registries reported 581,120 weight-loss operations in 2024.‡
*Deutsche Welle, February 2024   †ASMBS, June 2024   ‡IFSO, September 2026

Common procedures
  • Sleeve gastrectomy (gastric sleeve)
  • Roux-en-Y gastric bypass
  • One-anastomosis gastric bypass
  • Biliopancreatic diversion with duodenal switch
Before you travel
  • Have a visit with your home doctor, if appropriate.
  • Choose your doctor abroad and complete your consultation. Arrange for your records to be sent securely and digitally to their office.
  • Discuss the plan for follow-up care with your home doctor and your doctor abroad.
  • Make sure your travel documents are in order and you secure travel insurance before your trip.

Planning your medical travel abroad

Questions for your doctor abroad
  • Is any special preparation needed in the weeks leading up to surgery?
  • How long should I plan to stay in the area after surgery, and are there any special instructions for my trip home and once I’m back?
  • Who will handle my follow-up appointments and nutrition support after I return home?
