Traveling abroad for heart care

Heart bypass, valve replacement, angioplasty and stents: what to know before you book your trip.

The care you need and the getaway you want, both in one destination.

Why choose medical travel?

Many people choose heart care abroad for the licensed doctors and accredited facilities, shorter wait times, and lower costs than they might pay at home.

A heart bypass surgery in Spain can cost $15,200 or less, compared with $89,000 or more in the U.S.*

Hospitals in the European Union performed 1.11 million coronary angioplasties in 2023.†
*iFHP and Health Care Cost Institute, January 2025   †Eurostat, February 2026

Common procedures
  • Coronary artery bypass (heart bypass)
  • Heart valve repair or replacement
  • Coronary angioplasty and stents
  • Pacemaker or other device implantation
Before you travel
  • Have a visit with your home doctor, if appropriate.
  • Choose your doctor abroad and complete your consultation. Arrange for your records to be sent securely and digitally to their office.
  • Discuss the plan for follow-up care with your home doctor and your doctor abroad.
  • Make sure your travel documents are in order and you secure travel insurance before your trip.

Planning your medical travel abroad

Questions for your doctor abroad
  • Is any special preparation needed in the weeks leading up to my procedure?
  • How long should I plan to stay in the area after my procedure, and are there any special instructions for my trip home and once I’m back?
  • Who will handle my follow-up appointments and cardiac rehab after I return home?
