Traveling abroad for wellness and health checkups

Health checkups, wellness retreats, and traditional therapies: what to know before you book your trip.

The care you need and the getaway you want, both in one destination.

Why choose medical travel?

Many people travel abroad for a health checkup, a wellness retreat, or traditional therapies, looking for licensed practitioners and accredited facilities, and to combine their appointments with time away.

Travelers took 1.34 billion wellness trips worldwide in 2025.*
*Global Wellness Institute, November 2026

Common packages
  • Preventive health checkups and screening packages
  • Wellness and recovery retreats
  • Alternative and complementary therapy packages, such as chiropractic, traditional Chinese medicine, or Ayurveda
Before you travel
  • Have a visit with your home doctor, if appropriate.
  • Choose your doctor abroad and complete your consultation. Arrange for your records to be sent securely and digitally to their office.
  • Discuss the plan for follow-up with your home doctor and your doctor abroad.
  • Make sure your travel documents are in order and you secure travel insurance before your trip.

Planning your medical travel abroad

Practical tips
  • Decide what kind of wellness package you want—preventive tests and screenings, a wellness retreat, or alternative therapies—and weigh all your options.
  • Check what each package actually includes, and what costs extra.
  • Confirm how practitioners are credentialed or licensed, where that applies.
