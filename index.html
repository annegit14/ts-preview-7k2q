Planning Essentials

  • A conversation about your medical travel needs and the countries you’re considering for care
  • A pre-trip planning guide
  • Questions to ask your doctor abroad
  • Personalized follow-up email with suggested next steps

Guided Coordination

  • Everything in Planning Essentials
  • Requesting quotes and package details
  • Scheduling appointments
  • Completing administrative intake forms
  • Sending your medical records and other forms to your doctor abroad
  • Travel suggestions for flights and lodging, which you book yourself

Full Concierge

  • Everything in Guided Coordination
  • Booking your flights, lodging, and transfers
  • A dedicated contact by phone, text, or email from your first conversation with us until you return from abroad
  • Arranging translation abroad and coordinating your follow-up care once you’re home

Logistics only. We do not give medical advice or travel with you.
